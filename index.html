Building physics and simulation engineer with 30 years' experience. Started in aerospace and defence analysis at DSTO and offshore blast modelling at BMT, spent ten years at Arup, including Australasian building physics leadership, and later eight years co-directing an independent ESD practice that delivered rating and compliance submissions for councils and developers. Tests buildings against heat, wind, daylight and energy performance to find where they break down, and builds the tools that make results accurate, consistent and repeatable across every project. Now focused on climate resilience work that asks how buildings will cope with future climate, not only how they perform under today's conditions.
